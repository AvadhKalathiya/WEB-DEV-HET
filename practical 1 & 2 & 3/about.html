About CHARUSAT

About University

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a leading private university located at Changa, Anand, Gujarat, India. The university provides quality education in various fields of Engineering, Technology, Pharmacy, Management, Computer Applications and other disciplines.

Vision

To become a globally recognized university by providing excellent education, research opportunities and innovation-based learning.

Mission

To provide quality education, develop professional skills, encourage research and create responsible citizens for society.
